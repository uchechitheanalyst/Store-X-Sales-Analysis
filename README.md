## Insights
- In 2023, VIP customers became the third revenue generators with an increase of 0.90% from the previous year, displacing business customers who then became the fourth revenue generators.
- Revenue declined from 2022 through 2025 by 1.62%, 0.02%, 0.22%, and 0.59% respectively.
- Business customers generate the highest return on sales. 
- In 2021 and 2024, Canada generated the third least revenue after UAE and India; in 2022, 2023, and 2025, Canada generated the fourth least revenue after UAE, India, and Australia.
- In 2025, Sports and Outdoors experienced a decline in sales by 6.01%, making it the third top-selling product after previously ranking first from 2021-2024.
- 2024 recorded the highest return on sales of 36.28% and the second least revenue generation of $27.72M; a decline of 2.34% and 2.18% in the sales of Automotive and Electronics, the product categories with the least return on sales was experienced.
## Limitations
- The store’s return policy was undisclosed, hence, returned products were excluded from the analysis.
- The irregularities in the discount rate on each sale made it impossible to factor it into the analysis.
## Interpretations
- Considering the store’s location to Canada, the sales experienced in Canada is minimal.
- Revenue generation does not translate to a healthy return on sales.
## Recommendations
- The management should reach out to business customers and identify the factors contributing to a decline in their patronage.
- Advert placements and campaigns should be focused on Australia, India, and UAE as they promise to have leads.
